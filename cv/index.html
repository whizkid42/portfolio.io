Professional Summary

Robotics engineer focused on medical devices and surgical robotics, with hands-on experience taking electromechanical systems from concept to tested prototype. At IIT Madras Research Park I developed the force-control and haptic teleoperation stack for a 7-DOF robot-assisted ultrasound system and built the test phantoms used to validate it. My background spans control (admittance/impedance, haptics), mechanical design and fabrication, sensing and perception, and system architecture with risk analysis for regulated devices. Based in Bengaluru and looking for R&D roles in medtech and surgical robotics.
