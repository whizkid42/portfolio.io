**Healthcare Technology Innovation Centre (HTIC), IIT Madras Research Park · 2026**

Patients breathe, so a robot that scans or treats the torso has to cope with a surface that keeps moving. I built a breathing phantom simulator that reproduces respiratory motion on a tissue phantom. It gives us a repeatable way to test how the [tele-ultrasound robot]({{ '/projects/1_project/' | relative_url }}) tracks and holds contact on a moving body before it is used on people.
