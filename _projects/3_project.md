**Hourglass Medical · Robotics Systems Design Intern · Champaign, IL · May – Aug 2025**

Surgeons and nurses keep stopping to reach up and re-aim the operating light. This project automates the job: ceiling-mounted robotic arms position the surgical lights on their own, using perception to track the surgical site. Smart glasses with a laser pointer let the surgeon point to exactly where the light should go, hands-free.

### What I worked on

- **System architecture:** led the architecture design using MBSE (SysML), defining the kinematics, perception, control and actuation interfaces.
- **ROS 2 software:** implemented perception and planning nodes, with real-time CAN bus communication to sensors and actuators.
- **Kinematics:** modeled and optimized the multi-DOF arm kinematics to improve positioning accuracy under real-world constraints.
